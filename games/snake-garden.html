SNAKE GARDEN

Guide your garden snake to eat berries and grow. A golden berry appears now and then: grab it before it wilts for a big bonus.

Every 5 berries a rock lands in the garden and you speed up. Don't hit rocks, walls, or yourself.

←↑↓→ or WASD to steer, or swipe on touch. P pauses.
